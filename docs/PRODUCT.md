## Who We Build For

### Primary Users
**Indian family caregivers** — typically the adult son or daughter who keeps the paper prescriptions, lab reports, and discharge summaries for parents and themselves, and has to make sense of them between doctor visits.

### Secondary Users
**Hackathon judges** at Altrix Labs, scoring across AI accuracy, architecture, UX, healthcare safety, and live demo quality.

---

## Product Purpose

Turn a photo or PDF of a medical document into **structured, verified facts** — medicines, doses, test values, diagnoses, dates — explain them in plain **English, Hindi, or Telugu**, and file them into one timeline per family member.

**Success looks like**: A caregiver understands what a report says and what to ask the doctor, without the app pretending to diagnose.

---

## Positioning

Every extracted fact stays **traceable to the original document** (shown side-by-side) and to an **ABDM-aligned FHIR R4 record**, so the plain-language layer never floats free of the source.

## Operating Context

| Context | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Input Sources** | Paper documents photographed on a phone, printed lab PDFs, bilingual Hindi/English prescriptions, handwritten Rx |
| **Usage Environment** | Used at home, often on mobile, sometimes read aloud to elderly parents |
| **Demo Environment** | Runs on a laptop for judges during live hackathon evaluation |

## Capabilities & Constraints

| Capability | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Frontend** | Next.js 15 App Router with responsive design |
| **Backend** | FastAPI (Python 3.12) |
| **Vision OCR** | Gemini 3.8 Flash for extraction from images |
| **Text Fallback** | Groq Qwen for PDF text extraction and copilot |
| **Voice** | Groq Whisper for speech-to-text |

| Constraint | Detail |
|---|---|
| **ABHA Linking** | Mock implementation — no real ABDM gateway calls |
| **Data Integrity** | Never fabricate medical data. When extraction fails, say so. |
| **Scope Boundary** | Not a diagnostic or treatment tool; no dose-change advice |

---

## Evidence on Hand

Sample family records are generated by running the real extraction pipeline on **synthetic documents** and are labelled as sample data. No real patient data, testimonials, or clinical validation exist — we do not claim any.

---

## Product Principles

| # | Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Source First** | Every value can be checked against the original page |
| 2 | **Honest Uncertainty** | Show what the AI could not read instead of guessing |
| 3 | **Plain Language, Three Languages** | Same facts in English, Hindi, and Telugu |
| 4 | **One Timeline Per Person** | Never mix family members' records |
| 5 | **Explain, Never Diagnose** | Prepare questions for the doctor; never prescribe |

---

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Designed for older readers and low-literacy users:
- **Large, readable typography** with high contrast
- **Read-aloud support** via Web Speech API
- **Full parity** in Hindi and Telugu — not just translated labels, but culturally meaningful explanations
- **Mobile-first design** — works on small phones with touch-friendly targets
